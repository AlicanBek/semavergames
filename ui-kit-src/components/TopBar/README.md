The header of every menu screen: who the player is and what they hold.

Left: avatar, name and level bar. Right: currency pills. The name truncates with an ellipsis; the pills never shrink.

You provide: the display name, an avatar (initial letter until the player picks a photo or frame), the level number and progress from 0 to 100, and the balances.

The game logo is not part of this bar. On a phone it sits on its own line below the bar or is left out; it must never overlap the avatar. On inner screens (shop, settings) replace the identity block with a ghost "Geri" button and the screen title.
