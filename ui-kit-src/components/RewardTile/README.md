One day in the seven-day login reward strip.

Three states: claimed (cream inset, dashed, check mark and "Alındı"), today (red, raised) and upcoming (green). State is carried by the word and the mark as well as the color.

You provide: the day label ("3. gün", or "Bugün" for today), the reward amount with its unit, and the state.

The strip scrolls sideways inside its container on phones; tiles keep a fixed 72px width and are never squeezed. Place the strip inside a `Modal` or a panel with a single primary button under it ("Ödülü Al").
