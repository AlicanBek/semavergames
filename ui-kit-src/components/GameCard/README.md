A lobby card for one game mode or destination: title, illustration, one line of description, one button.

Variants: default cream; `sg-card--night` for the single featured card in a lobby (the shop); `sg-card--locked` for modes that open at a level.

The button on a cream card is the faceted button (`sg-btn sg-btn--hex`); a locked card shows it disabled with the unlock condition as its label. The night card keeps a ghost button.

You provide: a title of one or two words, the illustration, a description of up to five words, and the button label. The art slot is 88px tall; the suit glyphs in the preview are placeholders for the game's own illustration.

Cards sit in one row that scrolls sideways on phones, with the next card peeking at the edge so the scroll is discoverable. At most one night card and one ribbon per row.
