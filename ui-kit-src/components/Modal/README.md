A cream dialog over the dimmed felt, for one question or one reward.

Title, diamond rule, one or two sentences, then at most two buttons: the primary on the right, the alternative on the left as secondary (gold).

You provide: the title (a question or the name of the thing), the body copy, and the actions.

Rules:
- Every modal has a close button, and Escape and a tap on the scrim close it too.
- One modal at a time, and never more than one unprompted modal per session start. Rewards that are waiting show as a `Badge` on the nav instead of opening on their own.
- Maximum width 420px; on a phone it takes the full width inside the 16px gutter.
- Destructive confirmations use a `sg-btn--danger` action and name what will be lost.
